WHEN THE SKY CLEARS - MAIN GROUND IMPACT v014
Prepared design and native builder; this file does not establish a native build.

The retained v003 rises as a detached soft cloud; v013 is a compact puff that
is too small at shot scale. This candidate instead draws an asymmetric fan of
ripped soil, separating diagonal inner folds from near/far dust traveling along
the ground. Its widest solid fan is about3.2m, max height1.8m; the later thin
ground dust reaches4.6m. World placement and event start remain the existing
v003 origin and frame1824. Ten distinct chips have their own flight, landing
and settling times. Six landings receive brief dust brush exposures. Broken
ground marks remain after the airborne shapes finish.

The visible forms are editable native filled contour meshes. Eight hidden real
Grease Pencil contour guides are available for editing the main silhouette
sources. Every onset/expansion contour is an independently authored drawing;
the effect does not scale a complete smoke painting or simulate a fluid volume.
The original project-owned imagegen v003 smoke atlas is reused only as pigment
variation inside the contour geometry. No external assets or installs.

scripts/build_main_impact.py expects Blender5.2.2 with v047g opened from disk.
It saves a new context copy and a clean append collection only in this folder.
The context disables old FX rendered geometry while retaining the existing
physical v043 light and the original reflected-light receivers. Append asset
has no light to avoid double lighting; reusing it needs explicit receiver/light
integration. Original v047g is checked for unchanged SHA256 after saving.

Review keys to render in native4K: 1823,1824,1825,1826,1828,1830,1833,1837,
1842,1850,1860,1873,1892,1920. Judge separation, attack strength, child occlusion,
whether near ground dust looks cut out, and whether scar/landings actually read
through the painted terrain mask. Do not integrate on technical build success.
