ANCHOR / CONTROLS
Select 'FXv013 | terrain anchor and controls'. The original shot placement is
X 109.2294693, Y 10.0, Z -0.5300196 metres. Move this anchor for placement.
X is screen-right, Z is up; the drawing lies in X/Z and the camera looks +Y.
Metric scale: 1 Blender unit = 1 metre. The widest early torn silhouette is
about 1.07m wide; the debris spreads about 1.9m. The held camera sees roughly
205 pixels per metre at this depth in the 1920x1080 proof.
start_frame = 7: first impulse frame. Change to offset the whole effect.
speed = 1: age = (scene frame - start_frame) * speed. Use positive values.
size = 1: uniform scale around the terrain anchor; use this instead of editing
the scale fields, which are driven. intensity = 1: material opacity multiplier;
0 to 1 is the intended range. painted = 1 selects the original pigment finish;
0 exposes the plain color construction. Color is editable in the clearly named
FXv013 materials' palette inputs. Geometry is native editable mesh/shape keys.
The 'age' property is driven; do not key it directly. The asset starts before age
0 as invisible, and retains settled small debris after the short impact action.
Frame 1–72 at 24fps is supplied; first impulse is frame 7; final frame is age 65.
Ground variation is authored for this measured shot. Moving/scaling the asset
does not automatically solve new terrain. Adjust chip shape keys/contact drawings
for another slope, and place the collection behind foreground/character layers.

RGBA PLATE
rgba/Drawn_Compact_Impact_24fps/0001.png through 0072.png are full-frame,
1920x1080, 8-bit RGBA PNGs at 24fps. PNG alpha is straight/unassociated.
