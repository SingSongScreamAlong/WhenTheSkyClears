MOTION
The compact impulse arrives immediately, with most expansion in the firstfewframes. Left/right masses travel differently, lose upward/outward momentum and roll down; the low connected body and dust then spread and settle. Chunks have independent ascent/apex/descent and stop at ground contact; short puffs accompany several landings. The shallow gouge and landed debris have no end fade. Only late residual smoke/dust dissipates. No chimney is continuously sourced. There are no generated-pose crossfades, camera shake or audio emphasis.
