WHAT CHANGED
The old full painted plume is now only a short launch jet. Thirteen cold
smoke elements use four different original forms, separate births, rise,
roll, unequal mesh curl, shader advection, evolving erosion and lifetimes.
Earlier elements die while later elements change the contour and interior.
The flash, launch jet and held preview light response are exactly zero from
age 14 onward (frame 1838 here). Smoke and dust continue independently.
The ground-origin empty stays fixed. Native debris retains its trajectories.

All 120 new PNGs, 96 reference PNGs and both full MP4s decoded successfully.
GPU motion render took 55.18 seconds, one job, two CPU threads, 32 Eevee

The two targeted defects are corrected in the reviewed frames: late hot core
is absent and the smoke replaces its contour/internal grouping rather than
stretching one entire plume. Treat this as a candidate for the separate
integrated battlefield proof, not overall cinematic acceptance.
Four underlying painted forms are reused; this is 2.5D layered flow, not a
fluid simulation or true evolving volumetric self-shadow. The cloud lifts
clear of the dust, becomes broad, and thins strongly near the end of the
five-second test. Moving camera, live occlusion, character interaction and
4K sampling still need review in production's separate integrated proof.
