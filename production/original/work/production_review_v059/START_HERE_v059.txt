WHEN THE SKY CLEARS | PRODUCTION REVIEW AND BENCHMARK v059
18 October 2026

ASSESSMENT
The painterly village-to-devastation progression, charcoal children and continuous lateral journey give this project a recognizable identity. It remains in animation development. The largest quality gap is purposeful paired performance and final composition. Arcane-level production quality has not been attained.

v058d: newest packed world/foreground development, with improved continuous soil and school facade work. Its children remain held.

v036/v037/v038: separate locomotion and character studies. v037/v038 garment work has only five held poses; it is not a complete moving replacement.

PRIORITIES, IN ORDER
1. Full paired acting. Author the transition from individual play to caution, protective hunkering, running, fatigue and mutual reassurance. Feet must plant and release with root travel; pelvis, shoulders and gaze must lead and overlap deliberately. A single repeated walk cannot carry the story. The recovery still ends in a hold; author the first resumed step and its transition.
2. Final title staging. Render acting through the actual moving letter mask. Correctly evaluated v059 samples show heads, feet and parts of the interaction cut by the lettering. At frame 1918, the girl and contact are concealed. Stage key beats in clear openings or develop a separate treatment that allows those beats to read. An attractive unmasked shot does not establish a successful final composition.
3. Coherent moving drawings. Round the cuff openings and elbow construction, preserve sleeve volume, and redraw forearms as connected forms. Register head mass, hairline, neck and braid through the turn. The current rise still feels hinged; the girl's free hand remains posed behind her. Finish a complete garment cycle before integrating the newer static costume studies.
4. Directed lighting and depth. Preserve the warm-to-cold progression. Quiet detail behind faces and hands, use a restrained sky separation and source-connected firelight, and break the uniformly bright path into designed light areas. Build thickness only where camera parallax, contact and occlusion reveal it. Many source paintings are below UHD resolution.
5. Effects and finishing. Use a rooted impact, force-directed debris and persistent disturbance. The new painted eruption fits the world better, but its striated fan can read as wet/sculpted sheets and holds too long before settling. Simplify flame shapes, improve debris/ground contact, then finish cloth, braid, atmosphere and sparse contact sound.
