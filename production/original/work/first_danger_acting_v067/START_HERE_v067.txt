CHANGES FROM v066
- The near-arm solver now keeps a stable elbow bend during walking. The girl's
  backward reach transitions continuously from natural walking FK into its final
  bend, avoiding an elbow pop when the wrist crosses beneath the shoulder.
- A short settling movement after the hands meet changes the linked wrists,
  shoulders and chest; the boy's body response follows the girl's later.
- The hand approach stays open longer, then the original native finger shapes
  close. A small cupped-palm corrective groups the fingertips. The clasp is angled
  rather than remaining a level bar between two arms.
- The free arms bend into a closer guard during the recoil and relax back toward
  their travel motion. Existing sleeves were refit to the actual changed joints.
- Head pitch responds to the offered hand and lifts again after connection.
- Two brief, offset blink events per child use native skin/ink eyelid drawings
  sampled from the existing shared skin painting, with the original color clock.
  A compression-only pilot was too weak at viewing size and is disabled in the
  final native. No new face painting or alternate design was substituted.
- The girl's attached braid has a restrained two-frame follow-through correction
  based on the source head movement, retaining its original pigment and UVs.
