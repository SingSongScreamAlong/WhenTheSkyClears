PROCEDURAL COLOR CONTROL
Object: V064 | global procedural color loss | BOTH children
Driven property: charcoal_progress
Editable parameters: fade_start_seconds = 0; fade_finish_seconds = 96.

The driver uses absolute frame time, t = (frame − 1) / 24:
u = clamp((t − fade_start_seconds) / (fade_finish_seconds − fade_start_seconds), 0, 1)
p = 3u² − 2u³

Both children share p. Pigment saturation is 1 − p, and the blend toward the reviewed v063 neutral charcoal values is p. There are no palette keyframes and no per-shot reset. The pair starts in full painted color, loses color continuously, reaches the detail-preserving charcoal endpoint at 96 seconds and holds it through the ending. Keep finish later than start when editing the two parameters.

The charcoal endpoint uses the reviewed v063 neutral ramp of the full-painted-source luminance, keeping painted facial features and cloth detail visible. Original UVs, alpha, silhouette, geometry and lighting source graph are retained. The older lit-charcoal branch remains preserved internally; it is not the final endpoint.

The color change belongs to the actual 100-second picture clock. It is separate from the existing final picture fade and does not grade the credit card.
