ARTISTIC ASSESSMENT / LIMITS
The actual stills show a more localized knee bend and less broad sleeve/cuff transitions. The hand separates somewhat better from the coat, especially close up. Passing feet still form a crowded combined silhouette, and the final charcoal view remains harder to read than separated diagnostic values. Technical straight axes do not by themselves make the performance natural.
The body/coat still reads as smoother graphic panels while head, collar, sleeves, trousers and shoes carry denser painted texture. That model/style mismatch and the coat's broad panel shapes remain substantial issues. The next bounded art test should establish a coherent shoulder/ribcage/waist/hem garment with the existing painterly material language, while keeping character identity and preferred rhythm.
This remains a boy-only repeated walking study. Distinct paired intention, fatigue, stumbling, credible supporting contact and a recovery-to-walking restart have not been delivered by this pass. A more convincing performance requires those authored intentions and transitions, along with further model and drawing refinement.
Actual native stills and decoded movie frames were inspected. Continuous video-player playback was not observed through the available tools. User/independent normal-speed judgment remains necessary. No claim of studio-level acceptance.
