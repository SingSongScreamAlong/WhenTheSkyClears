OBSERVED MODEL / STYLE PROBLEMS
1. The retained v015 painting uses one fabric language across collar, sleeves and coat. In the current rig, head/collar/sleeves/shoes retain strong painted texture while much of the coat is smooth Grease Pencil value panels. This makes the body read as a different drawing system, even when motion is preserved.
2. The coat's broad hem opening and several long dark panels can read as separate rigid flaps. Ribcage, waist and pelvis compression are not consistently legible beneath them. More moving stripes or extra lighting will not supply convincing mass.
3. v035 localizes knee bending but its pose-sample interpolation still allows several millimetres of shaft bowing. Knee volume remains broad and passing leg/shoe silhouettes crowd together.
4. Hands exist and retain the original painted gesture, but they occupy roughly11x15pixels at the shot camera. A small charcoal lift helps; crowded outlines and nearly equal garment values still suppress the palm/thumb. F20 far-hand occlusion by the near leg is intentional and must remain physically sensible.
