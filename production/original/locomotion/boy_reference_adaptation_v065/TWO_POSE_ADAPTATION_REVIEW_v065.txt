Anatomy and contact read
- The local age-ten static guide gives approximately 1.415m shod stature, 0.335m thigh, 0.322m shin, and 0.223m shoe length. At 563px rendered stature the expected thigh and shin axes are about 133px and 128px. Phase 6 axes: rear 132.85/126.49px and front 132.85/126.49px. Phase 8: trailing 136.01/120.21px and planted 133.03/127.02px. These are plausible construction guides, not a measurement of completed anatomy or a certified age-ten model.
- Phase 6 reads as a two-foot-contact stride, while phase 8 has one planted foot and a trailing lifted foot. The boots share a palette and design cues, but their volume/soles are too polygonal, and the trailing boot in phase 8 looks undersized. Knee-to-ankle lines are clear but too ruler-straight in the painted silhouette; neither pose conveys sufficient knee compression, heel rollover, or mass settled over the contact foot.
- The arms differ by pose, but hands are simplified prongs and the wrists and shoulders stay angular. The phase 8 body is nearly static above the hips; the change of support is not expressed through pelvis, torso, or coat. These are pose studies only; no time spacing, root travel, momentum, recovery step, or foot-slide validation exists here.

Visual assessment against the actual A pixels
- The v053 boy has specific painted hair, cheek/ear/nose structure, cloth folds and edge variation, coat thickness, and shaped leather boots. The point-authored redraw preserves approximate palette and proportions but reads as a flat vector mannequin. The main board makes the loss obvious at shot size. Its source-faithful leg construction is useful, but it is not a faithful character rendering and is not a visual upgrade over A.
- Reusing the approved A head for a diagnostic retains recognizability, but exposes how far the simplified coat, arms, trousers and boots remain from the painted design. It also creates a mixed-media edge at the collar. This does not justify interpolating these poses.

Next development gate
Author one fully painted child support pose at the 660x720 comparison camera using the v049/v053 painted design as the visual target and the phase-8 segment guides only as underdrawing. Shape the coat and bent cloth around the actual weight-bearing hip/knee, paint individual fingers, and draw the heel/sole roll at the planted boot. Match it against the actual A frame at shot size before any second painted pose or inbetweens. Keep the retained hair/face continuity intact. If the painted pose passes, make the complementary stride and only then test two-pose timing/contact; derive any eventual world travel curve from the final root/foot tracks. No integration contract is claimed by this two-pose proof.
