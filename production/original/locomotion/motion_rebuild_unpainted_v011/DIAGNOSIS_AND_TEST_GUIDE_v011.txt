WHAT WAS WRONG IN THE INHERITED MOTION
Actual v010 rendered poses/streams, joint lengths and world-contact samples were
inspected. Hip heights remain about0.54-0.56m in the late walk, substantially
below the original rig's possible upright support height. Long bent-knee poses
therefore read as a crouched mechanical shuffle instead of walking over support.
Large torso rotations and separate drawing/foot trajectories obscure where
weight arrives, compresses and is released. Correct sole lock did not fix this.
The original boy has0.2898m upper arm and0.1265m forearm, encouraging elbow loops
and wrist reversals in the existing construction. Hand/body gestures could not
rescue the underlying weight/timing. The two children shared too much travel
and phase logic. v010's later foot swing speed/body speed ratio was about2.67;
that ratio by itself is NORMAL evidence of neither good nor bad gait, because
a planted foot has to regain body travel during swing. It is not treated as a
biomechanical failure test. The observed low hips, held torso and poor load
response/acting were the actual artistic problems to address.

THIS TEST CHANGES THE FOUNDATIONS
No painting, native v010 curves, source rig, production camera or FX imported.
New plain jointed forms preserve approximate child heights, large head sizes,
body proportions and total reach; internal elbow placement is more balanced.
Heights remain boy1.45m,girl1.3699m basis. This is not a new approved design.
Boy and girl have separate root curves. Camera follows their average, so neither
child is forced to keep the same route or phase as the other.
The boy's swing toe scuffs, lowers into support, then the other foot takes a
fast catch step. That landing compresses him before recovery. His pelvis then
travels over the planted front foot as he rises; the trailing heel rolls up
before toe-off and restart. There is no stationary body posed over moving feet.
The girl brakes with a shorter step, a smaller compression and a different arm
reaction, waits while the boy recovers, then resumes on a different contact clock.
Regular restart steps are about0.25m between contacts, with3.4-4.2cm swing
clearance. The emergency catch is deliberately faster/longer, not a walk cycle.
Walking hips return toward upright extension and load down after contact.
Spine segments and head have a small lag/counter-response instead of one rigid
whole-torso hinge. Arms follow continuous joint angles with contralateral swing;
hands do not reverse at the wrist. No backward head turn or load-bearing clasp.

REFERENCE PRINCIPLES AND LIMITS
Hof2007,The equations of motion for a standing human reveal three mechanisms
for balance,doi10.1016/j.jbiomech.2005.12.016:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16530203/
Used as a general balance-design principle: ground support and counter-rotating
body segments matter; an external helping force is not needed for this test.
Wang&Srinivasan2014,Stepping in the direction of the fall,doi10.1098/rsbl.2014.0405:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25252834/
Used as an inference for authoring: foot placement should relate to body
position/velocity rather than independent decorative limb arcs. That study's
data are adult steady-state/lateral walking, not validation of this child trip.
The mass dot is an approximate geometric mass proxy. No dynamics, forces,
calibrated child COM, motion capture or physically correct recovery is claimed.
Dynamic walking/falling can have COM outside static support; no false rule
requiring it to stay inside a foot throughout every frame is imposed.

REVIEW GATE
Technical integrity (joint targets, binding, fixed lengths, sole lock, floor)
is checked separately from whether the movement reads. The first proxy build
had a parent-space binding defect; it was caught in actual renders and fixed
before motion assessment. Failed evidence is retained as such, not delivered
as a motion result. Current native meshes are checked against joint centers.
The first valid poses show a readable two-stage catch/compression and separate
girl braking. Full-speed and shot-size motion still need artistic review.
The motion remains an authored stylized test: simple limbs, limited torso
twist/lateral balance, no clothing/hair dynamics or real grip. Do not transplant
it into painting based only on native/contact checks. Review this foundation
first; if it still reads badly, change timing/support rather than polish artwork.
