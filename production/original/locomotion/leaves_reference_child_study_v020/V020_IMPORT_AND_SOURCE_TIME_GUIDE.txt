LIMITS / REVIEW
This is an adult-reference study, not approved child acting. Pelvis accommodation
up to28.89mm, shin direction residual up to18.2deg, and a grazing swing boot remain
matching defects. The painted torso/knees/cloth remain unpassed as described in
V020_REFERENCE_TO_CHILD_REPORT.txt and V020_CORRECTIVES_NOT_STARTED.csv. No new
corrective art was authored. MP4 holds every pose0.5s for comparison; it is not
normal-speed walking. No broad in-betweens, finished walk or production/world
integration. Stop for independent review.
