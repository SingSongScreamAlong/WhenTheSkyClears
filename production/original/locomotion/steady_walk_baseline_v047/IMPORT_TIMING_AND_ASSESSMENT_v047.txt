WHAT WAS FOUND
Read-only cold evaluation of v046 found 48.6 mm pelvis fore/aft travel relative
to its linearly moving root. Pelvis world speed varied about .630-1.043 m/s.
Independent record review found a dip-rebound-dip around boy F17-19.75 and girl
F13.5-16.25, produced by reach-cap lowering of about 19.5-20 mm beyond the old
inherited height curve. Its pelvis height range was about 37-38 mm.
Foot rolls covered roughly -45 to +26 degrees, with different near/far swing
knee ranges. Held girl arms read as balancing/bracing rather than ordinary gait.
These are structural observations; hip projection is not whole-body COM.
The old cadence was REGULAR, about116 steps/min, with16/15-frame half-steps.
There is no diagnosis of random cadence or authored lateral three-dimensional
sway. v047's even half-steps are a deliberate baseline design, not a claim that
v046 had random timing. The cadence and speed changes are explicitly labelled.

WHAT CHANGED
An authored reachable pelvis/foot path replaces the inherited-motion reach cap.
There is one smooth pelvis rise/fall per half-step, with no extra local dip.
Pelvis fore/aft excursion is3 mm total; height range26 mm. Body lean is quiet:
boy1.45-2.15 degrees, girl1.85-2.55 degrees. Head pitch varies only.3 degrees.
Head vertical travel is about26 mm; its fore/aft excursion is about8.8 mm.
Foot roll is limited to8 degrees toe-up at landing and18 degrees at toe-off.
The foot comes through earlier after toe-off, with a smooth4.5 cm maximum sole
clearance. Near/far legs use the same ordinary pattern; no hyperextended or
backward knees. Source anatomy is preserved, not stretched to reach contacts.
Reciprocal arms replace the held bracing pose. Girl arm ranges/means differ from
the boy's and her gait leads2 source frames. No clasp/support intent is claimed.
All continuous cached sleeves and legs were regenerated from the new actual
bones. Existing painting, sleeve rolls, boot shapes and layering remain intact.
