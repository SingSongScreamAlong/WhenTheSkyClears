WHEN THE SKY CLEARS | MUYBRIDGE MANUAL SOURCE-ANATOMY TRACE | 2026-10-19

Source and method
The approved source is Animal Locomotion, Plate 2 (1887), phase 6, original
crop x=1581..1891 and y=32..620 (310x588 pixels). The original source SHA-256
matched the recorded PHOTO_SOURCE.json. The study uses only the adult source
pose: no child proportions, invented inbetween, or walk-cycle timing. Every
trace vertex in reference/phase06_manual_contours.json was placed by visual
inspection of the source pixels. The only formula maps those listed pixel
coordinates into Blender camera space; it does not generate anatomy.

Technical validation
Blender 5.2.2 LTS called the temporary native_curve_author.py operator once
per contour. The saved file contains 16 separate Grease Pencil strokes and
156 point positions on layer Phase 06 Visible Contours, frame 1. Cold reopen
matched every specified point within 1.2e-7 Blender units. The native render
is transparent PNG; the contact sheet's center panel composites that actual
render on paper, and the right panel recolors only its alpha cyan over the
source crop at the same two-times pixel scale. The screenshot/contact sheet
is 1.14 MB and was saved privately as Library file
[PRIVATE_LIBRARY_REFERENCE_REMOVED].

Visual review
- The stride, rear-leg extension, forward shin and both complete feet read at
  the reference crop size. Leading heel near (230,538) and trailing toe near
  (86,539) line up with the observed ground band; the raised leading toe is
  retained. The crop does not cut the head or either foot.
- The torso and forward arm are too diagrammatic. Several contour segments
  cross at the shoulder/waist because the photograph's arm obscures the
  underlying body; these should be broken or redrawn from a clearer source.
- The head/face is low contrast in this plate and the trace resolves it as a
  polygonal profile. The fist is a wedge. Knee and thigh contours have long
  straight runs where the photo shows soft mass and joint changes.
- These defects would be conspicuous at character shot size. This is an
  editable registration/anatomy study, not approved character line art or an
  animation improvement. No second phase and no 120-frame batch was made.
