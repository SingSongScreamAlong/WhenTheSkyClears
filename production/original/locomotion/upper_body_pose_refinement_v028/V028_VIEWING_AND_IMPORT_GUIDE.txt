FOCUSED CHANGES
The loaded-to-passing trunk bends/releases under the preserved head, with direction changes
restricted to -1.6 through +.65 degrees relative to v027. It is not a larger whole-body lean.
The chest pivots at its upper attachment; the waist/coat bridges the preserved pelvis below.
Upper-arm swing directions and elbow flex are authored separately for near/far sides.
The near forearm reversal follows its upper arm by 1.5 frames; far forearm by 1 frame.
Hand direction follows the forearm by .75/.5 frames, with restrained relaxed wrist orientation.
This separates shoulder, elbow and wrist reversals instead of moving the previous arm pose
through one common torso transform. The original painted arms/hands and fixed limb lengths remain.
The hand/coat separation is clearer in the inspected F11/F27 passing poses and F20 front swing.

Coat changes follow the body corrections: upper attachment follows the chest, waist compression
is derived from the amount of trunk bend and is limited to 5mm. The additional hem response is
a damped follower of that trunk correction, layered over the retained v027 passive response.
There is no new independently timed flare, new material or texture-polish pass.
