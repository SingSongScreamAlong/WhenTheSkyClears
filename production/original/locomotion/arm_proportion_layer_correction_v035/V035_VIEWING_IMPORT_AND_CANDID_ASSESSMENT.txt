CAUSE OF THE CURVED LEGS
The bones were not curved or stretched. Cold native samples show two straight rigid segments connected at each knee; near thigh/shin lengths approximately 288.801/248.088 mm and far 233.829/286.662 mm, constant to numerical precision. V024/V027 trouser drawings instead used cubic Hermite centerlines with a shared averaged tangent through the knee. That distributed bend along the drawn thigh/shin and produced the rubber-tube appearance. These trousers have native pose shape keys, not weighted skinning or a subdivision modifier.

LEG CORRECTION
Only those two trouser drawing meshes' 31 shape samples are refitted. Their centerlines follow the actual straight thigh and shin directions, with an 18 mm local knee rounding region. Original painted UVs, cross-section widths and rolled cuff detail are retained. The final 9 percent blends into the retained shoe cuff, whose final ring is exact. The skeleton, animation curves, shoe paths, root and hip/knee/ankle positions are unchanged. At quarter-frame samples, shape interpolation retains up to about 5.3 mm deviation in the measured straight portions; this is not a claim that every interpolated contour is analytically rigid. Source maximum measured deviations in those same regions were 33–36 mm along the thigh and 19–24 mm along the shin.

ARM CORRECTION
Upper arm 218 mm; forearm 198.239 mm near /198.091 mm far. Original total reach 416.239/416.091 mm is preserved. Shoulder and wrist paths, hand matrices, hand X/Z shapes and gesture are retained. Native fixed-length IK re-solves the elbow on the original bend branch. Existing joint-dependent Geometry Nodes refit the sleeves/cuffs, with local elbow rounding scaled to the new split; original widths, UVs and paint remain. The far hand mesh is shifted 2 mm in depth only, and exposed far forearm depth is set to 22 mm, just ahead of its own far trouser plane. Neither is promoted into the foreground collection. Original near-side body/coat ordering remains.

LOCAL READABILITY / INTENTIONAL OCCLUSION
Texture-preserving neutral charcoal additions: hand+.010 linear RGB; exposed forearm+.007. No new lights or glow. At shot size, F11/F27 near hand median rises from 31 to 41 against adjacent garment approximately 31–32; this helps local separation but does not make small fingers clearly readable. F20 far hand remains fully hidden. Actual opaque-ID ablations show it returns when the near trouser is removed; this near-side overlap is retained. It is not a missing hand or a reason to force the far hand above the body.

CANDID ARTISTIC ASSESSMENT
The two-segment leg structure is clearer in these static comparisons, and the elbow/cuff break no longer sits so low. This does not establish that normal-speed movement feels better. Knees still look broad in the passing poses, the overlapping legs/shoes remain crowded, and the arm/coat design is still graphic and dark at shot size. The source's angular sleeve/shoulder edges and limited hand articulation remain. The temporary light view reveals structure; it is not a proposed final lighting/palette pass. User judgment is still needed before a cycle render. Paired acting, the girl's motion, load-bearing contact and recovery-to-walking remain separate unfinished work.
