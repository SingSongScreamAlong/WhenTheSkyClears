TRAVEL / EXACT COORDINATE CONTRACT
One Blender unit = one metre. X is travel, Z is up, camera views from negative Y.
Let q = native_frame - 1. Period =31 frames =31/30 seconds.
Stride S =0.9524437384576381m. D(q)=S*q/31. Speed=0.9217197468944884m/s.
Near contact: q=0+31n; far contact: q=16+31n. Steps are intentionally unequal in time.
Near stance lasts18.5frames; far stance19.25frames from its own contact.
Boots roll over their original painted sole polygon and have a single continuously active goal each.
Quarter-frame values F1-F94 are in V024_TRAVEL_AND_SUPPORT_QUARTER_FRAMES.csv (373 rows).
The table supplies root, free hip, pelvis/chest/head, both ankle world positions, pitches, contact vertices and anchors.
Exact interpolation and rigid-hull solve are in scripts/motion_model.py and scripts/v021_model.py.
Use those functions for off-grid evaluation; a CSV interpolation alone does not reproduce all nonlinear detail.

For world-travelling characters, retain D and move the camera consistently; the ground is stationary.
For centred characters, stationary stage foot position is A(q)-D(q), and the ground/world must scroll -D(q).
Apply the same world offset to all character controls, including unparented world foot goals.
Do not animate only V021 | CHILD_SUPPORT_TRAVEL: the world-space sole goals must receive the same placement transform.
Do not add a second root translation or independently ease the landscape while these contacts are planted.
To continue cycles, evaluate local pose at q modulo31 and add n*S to root AND world sole goals.
The native preview timeline wraps world travel when restarted; the floor ticks are not a seamless video loop.
F32, F63 and F94 are congruent character poses translated by S, 2S and 3S respectively.
