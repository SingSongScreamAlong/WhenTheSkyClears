WHAT CHANGED
The old separate rounded lower value panels and triangular side-flap drawing are hidden in this isolated copy. A single connected 561-vertex, 512-quad surface now links shoulder, ribcage, waist and hem. The side flare is moderated, the front seam/opening runs continuously from the collar region, and the hem has softened corners plus a small front opening. The original collar/shoulder painting, head/profile/hair, arms/hands, trousers and footwear remain separate retained source assets.
The garment follows the unchanged pelvis and neck positions in each selected pose. Its vertical min/max extent is fitted to the exact original coat extent for that pose, preserving garment length. One restrained waist fold derives from existing hip drop: compression is near zero in the contact/passing/up samples and strongest in F4/F20. It is not separately clocked flapping.
Broad smooth garment-local value turns describe the surface. The lower-front value adjustment stays in fixed cloth coordinates in every pose; it does not follow the hand and is not a glow, lighting effect or isolated hand halo.

RETAINED PAINTING
The painted variant reuses the larger original coat/fabric region of Pair_Painterly_Near_Age_v015.png, including its existing seam/placket language. A structured UV map follows the new garment rows and front seam. It does not tile a small grain patch, transfer the old silhouette as an image card, generate a new texture or replace construction with noise. A small number of perimeter UV points are pulled into opaque existing fabric to avoid empty source pixels. The original packed image bytes remain unchanged.
The native control V037 | garment review values | static pose selector has property painted: 0 for construction and 1 for retained fabric. Both saved variants share the same geometry. Its compression property is keyed only at the static review poses.

CANDID ARTISTIC ASSESSMENT
The inspected stills read as one attached garment more consistently than the previous smooth chest / triangular side flap / rounded lower-panel combination. The original fabric painting connects the coat visually to the collar and sleeves. This is a direction for review, not a declaration of final art or studio-level animation.
Remaining issues: the coat can still feel fitted and graphic in profile; the inherited triangular upper-trouser edge below the hem is visible at F20; passing feet remain congested; small hands still compete with dark clothing and trouser texture. No new leg/foot contour correction is concealed in this coat pass.
