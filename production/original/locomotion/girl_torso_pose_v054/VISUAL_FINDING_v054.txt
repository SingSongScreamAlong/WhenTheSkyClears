WHEN THE SKY CLEARS | v054 GIRL TORSO KEY-SHAPE STUDY

This is a failed visual test, retained for an accurate design decision. The
frozen v053 native is unchanged. No v054 native animation or inbetweens were
authored because the key poses did not improve the girl clearly at shot size.

The existing dress is one 65x17 painted mesh. Upper rows are chest-weighted;
lower rows are pelvis-weighted. At v053 F4-6 and F20-22 the dark near sleeve
overlaps the dress shoulder. The same dark painting across bodice and sleeve
masks small changes to the silhouette, even when the underlying arm moves.

Six actual Blender source/candidate key pairs were rendered at loading,
passing and push-off, for each half-step. The first translation-only contour
test was too subtle. The revised test narrowed loading shoulder/waist edges
independently, lengthened them for passing/push-off, and shifted the sleeve
cap to retain insertion. It retained dress collar rows0-2 and skirt rows43-64,
all original painted pixels, UVs, face/hands, age-ten scale and existing feet.
Maximum geometric dress displacement was16.16mm; no grid cells inverted.

The doubled crop board and the 1280x720 shot-size board show the result. The
revised loading shape is detectable at close range but remains barely visible
at shot size. The passing/push-off shapes are still essentially the same
outline. These are actual rendered static keys, not a normal-speed animation.
No clean export or mesh statistic should be interpreted as artistic success.

The next useful local approach is a visible, phase-specific painted shoulder
and waist treatment tied to the arm insertion, using the existing outfit as
the design source, then another small key-board test before interpolation.
Larger mesh squash alone risks changing the child's proportions without
making the acting readable. The exact ordinary-walk travel and v053 performance
remain available as the current independent baseline.
