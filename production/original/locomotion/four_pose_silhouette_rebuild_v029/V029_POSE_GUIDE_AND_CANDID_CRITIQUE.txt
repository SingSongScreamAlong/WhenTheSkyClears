MY CANDID ARTISTIC REVIEW OF THE ACTUAL RENDERS
Useful changes:
Passing now has a readable support leg under the body and a separately bent swing knee, rather than two bowed tubes.
The smaller painted boots and restrained cuff bands reduce the previous dense foot/cuff cluster.
Thigh and shin have more consistent fabric volumes, with a visible knee direction.
Loaded-down shows more knee flexion and forward upper mass than contact; up extends the support silhouette.
Arm/hand shapes are more deliberate and their cuff-to-skin proportions are less abrupt.

Still not good enough to generate a full cycle:
The trunk remains too planar/columnar; waist/pelvis articulation and rib-cage mass do not yet carry enough intent.
Some knee/calf bends still look graphic and angular rather than soft anatomy inside trousers.
The front arm gesture is clearer but can feel posed/reaching. The rear arm disappears against the coat in places.
The coat is still broad flat value panels, and the plain new body surface contrasts with the retained painted head/boots.
The contact and loaded-down poses do not yet have a sufficiently strong difference in whole-body attitude at shot size.
The smaller boots may now need proportion refinement against the retained head/coat design; size change is not itself a pass.
This is a more useful anatomical starting point than the previous micro-timing variants, but it is NOT accepted animation.
The next drawing work should resolve trunk/pelvis mass and soften the leg construction within these four poses,
before adding inbetweens, cloth polish or repeated-cycle rendering.
