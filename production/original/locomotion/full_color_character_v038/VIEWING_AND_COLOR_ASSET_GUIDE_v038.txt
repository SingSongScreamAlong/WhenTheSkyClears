Only the upper contour field on the two trouser drawings is changed: the hidden root is rounded into a cap, upper-hip width is tucked, and the factor blends back to the inherited contour before 30 percent down the drawing. The dark before/after crops demonstrate the geometry repair independently of color.
No joint, pelvis, drawing centerline, foot path or garment-length change was used to conceal the defect. Root edge movement is an intentional art change; do not claim every rendered trouser vertex is unchanged. Original rest vertices, pose-shape coordinates and UVs remain; the changed point attribute is disclosed in the evidence.

CANDID ARTISTIC ASSESSMENT
Color makes the hands, forearm joints and garment boundaries much easier to distinguish in the inspected stills. The larger retained fabric region keeps coat/collar/sleeves in one painted language, and the root cap removes the sharp hip projection without moving the poses. This is a usable color study, not just an anatomical overlay.
The face/eye design, profile planes and arm/hand contours remain stylized and simple; color does not finish those forms. The coat can still feel fitted and flat in profile, the brush pattern is conspicuous, and passing feet still overlap. Motion quality and moving material continuity remain untested here. No final character-design, palette or studio-level animation approval is implied.
