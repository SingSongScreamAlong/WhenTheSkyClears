WHAT CHANGED / WHY
Two visible causes were identified in the actual v026 rendered poses and source trajectories:
1. The upper body remains nearly columnar while the hips perform a late backward reset.
   The centered hip moves backward 36.659mm between F25.25 and F27.25 in v026.
   V027 spreads the transfer across the step; the same interval moves backward 5.298mm.
   Weight acceptance now has a clearer compression, a delayed upper-mass settle and an effort to rise.
2. The coat flare/tuck has its own phase-authored sequence instead of responding to the torso.
   V027 retains one preserved v026 coat design and derives the hem lag from the actual torso angle.
   A damped follower settles over twelve cycles before any output sample is taken.
   Its only input is torso motion; there is no separate sinusoidal flap or new texture pass.

Footfall timing, root travel, actual boots, world sole goals and character proportions remain v026.
The new hip/body spacing changes the leg poses, so the painted trouser centerlines are re-fitted too.
Their UVs, rolled-cuff design and final seven-vertex boot-collar row remain unchanged.
The original arm swing is carried with the torso; it has not been given a separate new timing scheme.
The original painted profile, hair and gaze orientation remain intact; the head follows the neck position.
No flat head card turnaround, design replacement or rejected v008 head patch is used.

EXACT TIMING / TRAVEL CONTRACT
One unit = one metre, Z up, +X travel, camera from -Y.
30fps native study. Period = 31 frames = 1.0333333333 seconds.
Stride S = .9524437384576381m. Nominal speed = .9217197468944884m/s.
q = native F - 1. Unwrapped root D(q) = S*q/31.
Native movie range F1-279 is nine cycles. F280 is the next bookend and is not encoded.
Near foot initial contact = F1 / q0. Far foot initial contact = F17 / q16.
Near stance lasts 18.5 native frames; far stance lasts 19.25 native frames.
Root and both world sole-goal X channels repeat with spatial offset; the boy pose controls repeat locally.
World sole position at q+31n equals first-cycle sole position plus n*S.

V027_TRAVEL_AND_SUPPORT_QUARTER_FRAMES.csv supplies q0..279 at quarter-frame spacing,
including D, centered world scroll, new hip/upper origin, chest direction and unchanged foot bone goals.
The native is authoritative for the actual sole mesh, its rocker geometry and interpolation.
Import the new body controls, trouser drawings and GP coat together with the existing rig/render dependencies.
Moving only the root object while leaving the separate world sole goals behind breaks contact.
Use one common placement transform for the root hierarchy and both separate world-space sole-goal objects.
For a centered character, evaluate body/feet relative to D and move the ground/world by the same -D.
If production already supplies D through a moving world, remove that common translation once, consistently.
Do not apply both native root travel and a second equivalent world/camera travel.

At 24fps production timing, preserve seconds:
native F = 1 + phase_q + 30*(film_frame - chosen_film_start_frame)/24.
Relative world travel from the chosen seam is D(native F)-D(1+phase_q).
Do not paste 31 native samples into 31 production frames at 24fps; resample the GP exposures and drawing keys.
The phase, seam and root placement for the 75-80second recovery shot remain to be authored and reviewed.
This file does not supply an approved recovery restart or a finished paired recovery-to-walk transition.
