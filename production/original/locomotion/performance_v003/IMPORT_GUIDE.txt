SPEED / ROUTE MATCHING
For uniform scale S and NLA time scale T, world distances multiply by S and
speeds by S/T. Skip at T=2 is0.30m/s. Stumble's entire distance curve retimes
together with the character keys. Native2x retiming is tested.
When parenting to a production route, use root_motion=0 and derive animation
phase from ground distance at foot depth. Do not add this root travel twice.
For a varying route, one constant NLA scale is insufficient. Bake phase from
signed accumulated ground distance divided by the clip stride. For stumble,
retime route and baked distance curve consistently; this one shot ends stopped.
