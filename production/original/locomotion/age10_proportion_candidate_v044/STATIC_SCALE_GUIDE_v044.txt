MEASUREMENT DEFINITIONS / UNCERTAINTY
Barefoot stature measures sole support to the skull vertex, with shoes and hair excluded. Head height is vertical chin/menton to skull vertex. The cranial vertex is hidden by the existing hair, so its position in each painting is an explicit drawing estimate, not an observed skull measurement. Candidate source-pixel anchors:
boy atlas crown(225,95),chin(328,403); one uniform scale .194/308m per source pixel.
girl original pair crown(985,137),chin(1064,280); one uniform scale .193/143m per source pixel. The girl's full original braid stays with her head; it is not counted as anatomical head height.
Hand reference is wrist crease to middle-finger tip with hand extended; a hand pose/drawing envelope may differ. Bare foot is heel to longest toe, excluding the shoe. Our heel/toe points are guides inside the chosen boot drawing, not a newly modelled bare foot.
Sitting height includes head, neck, torso and pelvis and starts at a seat surface. Stature minus sitting height is not hip-joint height. Knee-top-to-floor and buttock-to-knee are surface measurements, not femur/tibia lengths. Internal joint positions and tissue offsets above are authored estimates. They are not claimed as exact measured bone targets.
