What is delivered
Phase 6 is the independently preserved v062 file. Phase 8 is a second, contrasting editable native Grease Pencil file in this v063 folder. Both were drawn by selecting contour points manually from the retained Muybridge Plate 2 photograph. Phase 6 shows a wide passing stride; phase 8 shows weight over the planted foot while the trailing foot lifts. This is motion anatomy reference, not a child-character drawing or a timed walk cycle.

Exact source and registration
The source JPEG is locomotion/three_way_walk_test_v057/reference/Muybridge_Animal_Locomotion_Plate_2_1887.jpg, SHA256 d0178c822beeae7c86f9e780f2fd4f9e6309accc9a8f03b8f4696b01abab1911. Both Blender files convert a crop pixel (x,y) into local GP coordinates ((x-155)*0.01,(294-y)*0.01,0) and use the same 620 x 1176 orthographic source camera at scale 5.88. Their render alphas were composited on the exact source crops at 2x registration. The local phase-8 contour list is reference/phase08_manual_contours.json. No automatic tracing, pose synthesis or inferred inbetween was used.

Visual result and limits
At source scale, both poses register their principal contours and foot contacts. The phase-8 planted sole and airborne trailing toe are now located on the photo, after the initial lifted-foot outline was corrected from an enlarged pixel inspection. Phase 8 communicates the narrower stride and single-support moment without simply moving the phase-6 contours. The head profiles are deliberately simplified where the photographic edge is unreadable. The phase-8 hand is still a rough cue: it has the direction and location of the hanging fingers, but its little peaks are too regular as a standalone drawing. The shoulder-to-upper-arm transition and upper back remain incomplete because the source is blurred and occluded there. Phase 6 remains schematic around its hip and shoulder. These are credible rough source traces for silhouette/contact comparison, not faithful finished anatomical drawings. The adult photographic proportions, uniform line weight and absent painting are unsuitable as production child designs.

Why no full animation yet
Two stills establish neither contact timing nor the 75–80 s shot's root travel. The photographs were taken at distinct camera-panel positions; the 620-pixel difference between crop origins is not a usable character travel curve. Interpolating these contours would add unobserved poses and likely produce sliding or stiff deformation. The next production-level step must solve the children's own silhouette and design at shot scale, then author support changes and overlapping action with an explicit root/ground contract. This study should not be imported directly over the approved v045c art or used to override the v046 scene.
