V001 ARTISTIC AUDIT / ACTUAL RENDERED WALK PHASES / 2026-10-17

Reviewed eight actual Blender-rendered phases spanning all32frames, the
rendered crouch/reach extrema, and the actual copied-glyph readability study.
Representative review image: v001_walk_motion_audit.jpg.

The current faces, hair, clothing paint and near-age height relation remain
recognisable. This does not make the movement cinematic or finished.

Observed problems:
- Puppet-like rigidity in the torso, shoulders and head. Little anticipation,
  follow-through or changing line of action; weight transfer reads weakly.
- Both children share identical phase, amplifying a mechanical marching feel.
- Sleeve cutout fragments appear below/alongside the jacket and dress.
- Braid/head cutout joining reads detached in some phases.
- Knees can look crouched or pumping instead of a relaxed cinematic walk.
- Shoes use fixed held-drawing contact without a developed heel-to-toe roll.
- Larger crouch/reach bends expose severe sleeve/underarm silhouette faults.
- The lettering test deliberately occludes faces and bodies. It is a mask
  readability study, not proof of successful final trailer choreography.

Foot sliding distinction:
In-place shoes move backward relative to the camera at the moving floor's
speed by design. Native planted-contact drift after compensating for that
travel is negligible, and sole samples stay near floor level. These metrics
do not prove convincing screen-space weight or heel/toe behavior. A full
screen-space planted-shoe versus visible-ground comparison is still required
for v002. No claim of zero apparent skating or cinematic quality is made.

Response in v002:
Keep original face/body paint, author clean native flat overlap parts with
original sampled cloth/skin, keep the braid joined to the head, add deliberate
body/head timing and asymmetric phrasing, and develop only skip and one weary
stumble/recovery. Inspect rendered motion and preserve the earlier pack.
