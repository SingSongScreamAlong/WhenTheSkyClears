World route
Warm village and bakery -> abandoned village edge -> woods -> shattered school -> displacement camp -> battlefield and crater -> ruins and ash -> WHEN THE SKY CLEARS as physical lettering in the landscape.
Foreground trees provide depth and occlusion. The same camera tracks laterally, then widens and holds on the title. The held pair moves through the route without walking, running or crouching animation.

Art and edit
Ten set textures were made with built-in GPT imagegen: warm village, abandoned village, sky, woods, school, camp, battlefield, distant hills, ground and a complete foreground oak.
The pair is a separate imagegen design, converted to a manipulable native Grease Pencil vector outline.
Generated source paintings are around 1672 x 941 or 1536 x 1024; the native scene renders at UHD. Their files are preserved unchanged and packed in Blender. The two battlefield materials use alpha blending to remove the black overlap artifacts discovered in the v004 export.
The supplied 48 kHz stereo WAV is exactly 100.000 seconds. Its master volume fades from 90 to 100 seconds. The final picture fades over the last two seconds.
