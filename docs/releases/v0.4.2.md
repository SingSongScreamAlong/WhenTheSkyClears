## Changes

An optional two-child counterweight puzzle joins the existing woods route before the mill. Bring the boy onto the near pressure cradle and ask him to wait with **Q**. His actual weight lowers a hinged bridge; cross after it settles and step onto the far iron tooth. The ratchet can latch only while a different child holds the cradle. Then call him across and search the finite keeper's medicine. Releasing the cradle early raises the span above the safe lower road. The bridge's real collision, both children's floor contacts and the latch state drive the encounter; an interaction button cannot grant completion.

The girl gains a dedicated painted coat, upper/lower sleeves and boot atlas, mapped to the articulated rig while preserving her established head, hair and blue ribbon. Work now distinguishes pumping, bracing, clearing, searching, warming and balancing. Both children's hands follow actual moving pump grips. Physical pressure-cradle contact starts a sustained balancing pose; walking away releases it without locking the child in an action. Turn follow-through, landing anticipation, cold hands and quiet care develop the performance between hazards. The native pump fixture measured maximum rendered grip errors of **1.091 world pixels for the girl and 1.045 for the boy**, with **zero stationary body drift** during that fixture. The labelled normal-speed movie is linked below; user artistic acceptance remains separate.

Logging gates now lift gradually with their collision bodies. A released closing gate arrests above a child beneath it. The painted cradle, pulley, rope and opposed counterweights follow the actual displacement, and a far latch can preserve the open state. Generic barriers use timber slats, iron straps and support guides in place of the stretched doorway crop. Grass responds to nearby physical contact. These revisions keep the painted tent's material clarity as the benchmark.

The optional encounter uses the established woods route. Campaign length remains **79,200 world units across five chapters**; this version does not add another chapter or claim longer human playtime. Finite food, salvage and medicine, cover, timed listening lanes, shared work, route choices, shelter and the physical mill remain the survival baseline. Campaign saves remain compatible with v0.4.0.
