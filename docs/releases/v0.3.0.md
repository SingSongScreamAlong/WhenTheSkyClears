**When the Sky Clears — development 0.3.0**

Guide two children through five painted chapters, from a village to woods, a displacement camp and railway station, a ruined school, and an ash garden. Keep both sheltered, manage finite supplies, time exposed crossings, and work together to clear or lift obstacles. The expanded route includes six optional elevated supply paths, shared work, crosswinds, searchlights, falling masonry, and a flooded courtyard.

**Play:** extract `When_The_Sky_Clears_Windows_v0.3.0.zip` and run **When the Sky Clears.exe**. Godot and Python are not required. The separate editable-source ZIP opens in Godot 4.6 Standard.
