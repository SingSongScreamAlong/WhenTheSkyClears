## Three physical routes

**Orchard:** hold E while moving against the heavy crate to push it 130 pixels along its iron track. Genuine contact with the end chock loads the cradle and raises a hinged timber ramp. Both children climb the ramp to search one food and one salvage. Releasing the crate arrests its motion; recovery and saved progress preserve the mechanism's intended state.

**Freight lift:** lead the boy onto the real 160-pixel cargo deck and ask him to wait with Q. Return to the lower winch and hold E to raise him 170 pixels. His actual floor contact at the upper landing catches the load ratchet. Climb the freight steps, call him across and search one food and one medicine together. Crouch + E lowers an unlatched lift. Descent arrests above either child beneath the deck and resumes with the winch held once the path is clear.

The yard signal's shared control sits independently on the actual lower-road slope at x=13,690, with a 20-pixel vertical reach. Reunite at that grounded control to turn the patrol light away.

**Garden ferry:** approach the near jetty, board both children and crouch beneath the cloth. Hold E beside the reel to drive the actual rigid deck 450 pixels along the canal; releasing E brakes it. Each child needs their own floor contact and crouched position. Real far-dock collision moors the ferry. Step ashore together to collect the existing finite medicine reward. A complete lower wading route remains available, with wetness and cold as its cost. Native exit tests cover both the deck and the graded shoreline; checkpoints remain outside the hull corridor.

These are optional routes within the existing **79,200-unit, five-chapter journey**. The village, woods, camp/railway, school and ash gardens retain finite supplies, shared work, shelter, low cover, timed hazards and the need to protect both children. This version does not extend the route length.
