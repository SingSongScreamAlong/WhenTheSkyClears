## Character and animation reference downloads

Two **Quaternius Free Standard** downloads were inspected during development:

- [Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library). The original controller sampled selected stock joint motion into the game-specific [`child_motion.json`](../game/assets/motion/child_motion.json) bake. That earlier controller and the bake are retained for development history; the current child rig is authored in `child_v2.gd`.
- [Universal Base Characters](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters). The downloaded edition contains adult male/female superhero bases and hair options. These were topology and rig references, not finished child models, and do not render as either hero.

[Exact receipts](../game/character_sources/SOURCE_RECEIPTS.json) retain publisher pages, archive SHA-256 values and the inspected file lists. The two archive license texts declare CC0 and are copied unchanged here: [animation library](licenses/Quaternius-Universal-Animation-Library-Archive-LICENSE.txt), [base characters](licenses/Quaternius-Universal-Base-Characters-Archive-LICENSE.txt).

The [publisher license snapshot captured during development](../game/character_sources/QUATERNIUS_CURRENT_LICENSE.html) also contains the Quaternius Asset License (QAL), including a restriction on standalone asset redistribution and a clause about earlier versions. This repository preserves that record and links to the [publisher's license page](https://quaternius.com/license.html); it does not resolve the difference between the archive notices and the captured website terms. Raw vendor archives and extracted standalone model packs remain outside this public repository and its downloads. They are not required to play or edit the current game.

The original handoff also lists Blender Studio Human Base Meshes and Spring as possible future modeling references. Neither was downloaded or integrated, and they are not dependencies of this game.

## Native art review

The audio-close revision changes shutdown handling: active audio is stopped, streams released and a short asynchronous drain completes before exit. It adds no new sound assets or license changes.

v0.4.3 retains the raw boy-cloth and trolley-part atlases above, with their exact prompts, references and original generated paths. Both outputs were inspected as 1254 × 1254 RGBA. Their mipmaps are enabled, and the existing v0.4.1 interaction-prop atlas now generates mipmaps as well to reduce noisy distant sampling. These are import/runtime presentation changes; original source pixels remain preserved.

The [v0.4.3 board](../game/evidence/v043/V043_PLAYABLE_REVIEW.jpg) selects from **19 verified native 4K captures**, combining moving cover, dock and reunion with character/pump/woods and village/school/ash context. The silent [performance movie](../game/evidence/v043/WTSC_Character_Performance_Review_v043.mp4) passed full decode at **1440 × 810 / 30 fps / 522 frames / 17.4 seconds**. The [trolley movie](../game/evidence/v043/WTSC_Trolley_Playthrough_v043.mp4) passed at the same size/rate, **530 frames / 17.6667 seconds**. Their [performance](../game/evidence/v043/performance_movie_validation.json) and [trolley](../game/evidence/v043/trolley_movie_validation.json) records bind exact fresh frame ranges and native invocations to the current frozen executable.

The complete recorded 180-frame push kept at least **9.941 pixels** of forearm/face clearance; 159 settled live-contact frames had maximum grip error **0.484 pixels**. The 45-frame release kept **23.153 pixels** minimum clearance and a maximum shoulder step of **5.694 pixels**. These are bounded render-time measurements, separate from artistic acceptance. Trolley footage has an explicit near-bank setup before physical input; performance stages stance, warning, cold and cart contact. The frozen build passed 481 native functional checks and a separate 27/27 source animation fixture. Its five-chapter traversal completed in 730.4167 simulated seconds with one checkpoint recovery, final health 100 and no failure; that is not measured human playtime. Native checks, normal-speed viewing, human playtesting and user artistic acceptance remain distinct; AAA quality is still the target.

## Repository packaging

Runtime assets and source licenses are included. Windows executables and versioned source ZIPs are distributed through [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/SingSongScreamAlong/WhenTheSkyClears/releases). Local engine installations, import caches, save games, redundant capture frames and session-bearing download-page HTML are not source assets. The source receipts and provenance files remain available for review without publishing those machine artifacts.
