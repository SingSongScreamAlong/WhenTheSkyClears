WHEN THE SKY CLEARS — GAME DESIGN AND CHARACTER ASSET HANDOFF
19 October 2026

ADAPTATION
The handoff's two children, girl with braid and faded blue ribbon, smaller boy
with untidy hair, and warm-to-ash landscape progression guide this game. The
specific puzzles, resource economy and memory text are original game adaptation
material, subject to story review. No family relationship has been invented.
The survival direction follows the user's This War of Mine / Limbo references:
vulnerable traversal, scarcity, environmental storytelling and mutual protection.

EDITABLE GAME STRUCTURE
project.godot / scenes/main.tscn: native Godot project.
scripts/game.gd: journey, supplies, finite caches, save/load, hazards, companion,
  checkpoints and runtime validation commands.
scripts/child.gd: native CharacterBody2D movement and editable 2D character rig,
  retargeted animation blending, painted profile/garment registration and hand IK.
scripts/world.gd: painted parallax planes, props, ground, cover, weather and light.
scripts/hud.gd: menus, pack, controls, survival indicators and ending.
levels/*.json: five authored chapters, independent of the renderer.
tools/author_levels.py: editable source for route generation.
tools/compose_audio.py: original ambient loop and five procedural foley cues.
tools/bake_motion.py: glTF sampler, child proportion mapping and 2D motion bake.

ASSETS ACTUALLY INTEGRATED
Ten original workspace environment paintings are copied into assets/environments.
The original source files in sets/painted_layers are unchanged. Source rectangle
registration removes empty matte space during rendering without editing pixels.

assets/characters/boy_atlas.png is copied from the project's painted v040 atlas.
assets/characters/girl_profile.png is copied from the project's v050 girl profile.
The faces retain those established studies. These are fixed side profiles;
facial turns and new expressions are not supplied by those images.
The game's editable vector/texture rig supplies child proportions, arms, legs,
dress shape, braid/ribbon movement and a bounded paired hand contact.
The older simple drawing routine is retained as a fallback.

VERIFICATION
evidence/packaged_smoke/runtime_smoke.json: 73 engine checks, including each
chapter, native collision and movement, interactions, resources, recovery,
actual atomic disk save, resume readback and title-screen save preservation.
evidence/packaged_survival/survival_consequences.json: 13 consequence checks.
Covering either child alone leaves strength at 35 after a blast; covering both
leaves it at 100. Two open blasts end the attempt. Detecting the exposed boy
while the girl is sheltered leaves strength at 66. Food and cold consequences,
finite inventory, non-duplicating caches and reunion conditions are measured.
evidence/packaged_traverse/continuous_traversal.json: all five chapters and the
ending, with native physics and simulated input. No position jumps, collision
bypass, supplies grants or damage immunity. It uses the actual interaction
functions and the finite inventory. Zero falls/deaths in the automated route.
The targeted scenario tests explicitly set initial positions and conditions;
they do not claim a continuous playthrough. The traversal report does.
