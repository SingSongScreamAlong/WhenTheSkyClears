WHEN THE SKY CLEARS / 0.5.1 / LAST HOUSE VISUAL AND MOTION CHECKPOINT

This bounded pass refines the existing Last House section. It adds no chapter
or route and retains the v050 survival, stealth, interaction and save rules.
Choose Last House before the Crossing from the title. The full journey and
freight rehearsal remain available. Both children keep their original atlases,
identity, scale1.0 and equal99/74standing/crouched collision heights.

VISIBLE CHANGES
Last House movement has short start/stop/turn offsets, eased crouch depth and
landing compression. Idle stance feet keep their world placement rather than
sliding back into a symmetrical rest pose. A stance restarts from the current
foot positions, and turning uses actual travel direction for the stride.
These pose/foot changes are conditional on Last House. Native movement and
collision still own the children; there is no animation-driven root motion.
The initial gathering step uses the foot already ahead and its available travel,
then joins the normal stride. Separate planted/swing state preserves liftoff when
walk/run timing changes. The rendered pelvis fits both finite leg-reach disks;
regression measures actual hip, knee and ankle transforms through first steps,
sprint changes, stopping, reversing, crouching and landing for both children.
Discontinuous root moves resynchronize the planted pose instead of retaining
feet at the old position. Native tests cover this saved/debug setup boundary.

During support work both hands target the visible handle. The girl bends to
lower a cloth bag instead of immediately showing a crate. During the meal,
the children turn toward each other and bring small portions toward their
mouths; the boy attends while the bag is set down. Quiet relief uses a softer
standing pose. Paired hands use current rendered positions and a reachable
shared grip instead of a stale physics target stretching the leading arm.

The flat translucent band crossing room interiors and lower legs is removed.
Clothing, faces and edges read more clearly with restrained local warmth.
Sole shadows, selective roof-edge drips, wet-step flecks and nearby grass bends
give limited contact/weather feedback without covering the hands or silhouettes.
The existing painted assets are retained; no new raster character was invented.

REMAINING SHORTCOMINGS / NEXT SCOPE
The children remain procedurally articulated painted cutouts. These are authored
timing/pose refinements, not finished acting, final fingers, physical weight or
AAA animation. Some profiles/turns, elbow/knee silhouettes and cloth transitions
still need artistic judgment. The meal/bag gestures are brief; quiet relief is
standing rather than a finished seated performance. No completed external Godot
motion export was available; older review-only Blender rigs were not integrated.
Companion spacing during reversals can still stack silhouettes briefly and needs
a later movement/acting pass. Root navigation and pair collision rules are retained.

Repeated house imagery, generic foreground benches/props and parts of the wider
play plane still need bespoke scenery integration. Lighting/weather responses
are selective native drawing, not physical weather or a finished cinematography
pass. These refinements should be judged in motion at normal game size.
Audio spatialization/cues/mix/listening and accessibility/device prompts,
rebinding, text scaling and crouch toggles remain for the next pass.

is claimed. Scripted route times are not a5-10minute human experience estimate.
