WHEN THE SKY CLEARS / 0.5.0 / LAST HOUSE ROUTE CHECKPOINT

Open game/project.godot in Godot 4.6 stable Standard and run the project.
Choose Last House before the Crossing from the title. It resumes its own saved
checkpoint. Pause > Begin Last House again starts this section fresh. The full
five-chapter journey and freight rehearsal remain separate title options.
On Windows use PLAY.cmd or LAST_HOUSE.cmd; local release is build/v0.5.0.

This bounded section adds the painted abandoned house and photograph, bread,
an optional exposed shop shelf versus a low sheltered arcade, reciprocal help
through a damaged passage, railway islands with moving light and pair-sized
cover, and a dry room with bag-down, shared food and quiet playable relief.
Hold the support until he reaches the far catch. When he has it, walk right.
Releasing early lets him return safely for another attempt. Hold S to crouch
inside a wall and give the follower time to settle beside her before leaving.
Both children retain equal standing/crouched collider heights (99/74 units).

E performs a quiet interaction; HOLD E is shown for searching, bracing and the
support. Movement/jump/crouch/sprint, Q wait/call and H/LB medicine are retained.
GIRL/BOY COVERED, OPEN, SEEN and HURT report each child's state. Shared strength
still loses health if one child is caught. Seen/injury feedback identifies that
child. Covered followers target a safe slot inside the actual wall bounds.

Save isolation: Godot user:// is under WhenTheSkyClears/v050. Main journey,
freight rehearsal and Last House each use separate files. Original/v040/v041
folders are untouched. Native save tests use unique disposable filenames.
Finite caches retain supplies that do not fit. The shared meal consumes only
one ration and cannot be repeated for extra food. If no ration remains, the
room's emergency bowl allows relief with less nourishment. This is a recovery
fallback, not a reward equal to bringing food. R/checkpoint recovery preserves
collected supplies and completed work.

REMAINING POLISH / KNOWN LIMITS
Current procedural children remain angular. Bespoke starts, stops, turns,
crouches, landings, reciprocal hand/prop contact, carrying/bag-down, sharing and
quiet-window acting remain to be authored/refined. Quiet relief remains playable
with standing procedural poses; there is no finished sitting performance.
No external character-motion export was found or integrated in this checkpoint.
Character height/identity and original assets were preserved.

The house asset is reused in three stretches. Broader custom scenery, stronger
play-plane integration, subtler transitions/occlusion, roof/rain/fabric/grass
reactions and environmental story details remain art work. Rain is a simple
native line layer, not a finished weather simulation. Support motion and wall
transparency are functional visual feedback rather than final animation.

The existing procedural ambience/foley and warning cues remain. Spatial sound,
more specific interaction/weather cues, mix and subjective listening are pending.
Device-aware prompts, rebinding, text scaling, crouch toggles and other basic
accessibility improvements remain pending; current controls/settings are retained.
No physical controller feel, human audio review, human route-duration estimate or
Mac runtime verification is claimed. Editor source portability is audited; a Mac
application export is not supplied. This is a playable route checkpoint, not
completion of the approved art/animation/audio/accessibility pass.
