PAINTED INTERACTIONS
Six new props give searches and work their own materials: a ration box,
medicine satchel, canvas tool roll, cast-iron pump body, separate pump lever,
and warm lantern. The 1536 x 1024 RGBA atlas retains its transparent source
pixels; native source rectangles and transforms provide the runtime parts.
Its exact built-in ImageGen prompt and source provenance are recorded in
assets/props/Interaction_Props_PROVENANCE_v041.json.

Partially searched caches display what remains. If the pack is full of food,
taking medicine removes the medicine art while leaving the unclaimed food.
Empty containers remain as search history. Rendering does not grant supplies.
The pump lever rotates at its bearing toward the working hands. The sluice
rises with the receding water. Clearing, bracing and shared lifting receive
local work movement; warmer lanterns give braces a distinct focal point.

PLACES AND MECHANICAL FEEDBACK
School, railway and ash regions use more distinct architectural fragments
and staging. Cleaner banks, bridge edges and foreground vegetation leave room
to read the route. The mill refines its crop registration, chain, bearing and
breakaway debris while retaining its connected bridge/fence/wheel/gate sequence.
This pass refines the existing 79,200-unit, five-chapter journey rather than
claiming additional chapters or a new measured human playtime.

Work now interrupts if the child leaves its height, releases the input or loses
the required companion. Both children are released from interrupted shared work.
Pause holds work and the lever; inventory and pause hold the water transition.
The source fixtures test these states separately from full-journey navigation.
Existing finite food, salvage, medicine, cold, wetness, cover and both-child
requirements remain part of the campaign.

CONTROLS
A/D or arrows: move | Space/W/Up: jump | Shift: run | S/Down: crouch
Hold E: search, work or rest | Hold E+A/D against crate: push
Q: ask him to wait low / call him | H: medicine | Tab: pack/objective
R: checkpoint recovery | Esc: pause | F11: fullscreen | M: mute
Controller: left stick/D-pad move, A jump, B crouch, X held work,
Y wait/call, RB run, Back pack, Start pause.

BUILD AND EVIDENCE
From the repository root, run python game/tools/setup_godot.py, then
python game/tools/build_v041.py export. The helper temporarily selects portable
Windows templates and product metadata, then restores the preset byte for byte.
It hashes all runtime source, assets and UID sidecars. Manual editor exports
instead require matching Godot 4.6 templates installed through Godot.

After freezing the source, use the same helper with test all, test capture,
verify, and package. Output stays under build/v0.4.1 and evidence/v041. Earlier
v0.4.0 packages and evidence are preserved. The verification operation writes
nothing. Each scenario removes only its recognized direct outputs and records
fresh per-run output hashes, so stale JSON/PNG files cannot become new proof.

The 386 passing native checks include the earlier 371 and 15 refinement checks.
The 27-check source animation fixture includes the 13 baseline movement checks
and stays outside that total. The 12-image review includes staged pump-work,
fully drained sluice and supply close views. Continuous navigation completed
all five chapters with no failure, one checkpoint recovery and 730.45 simulated
seconds. That is an automated route result, not measured human playtime.
