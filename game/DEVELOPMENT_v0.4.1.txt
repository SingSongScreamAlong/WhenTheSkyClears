WHEN THE SKY CLEARS / 0.4.1 / TRUST FIX CHECKPOINT

This checkpoint fixes the five reproduced audit failures before the larger
Last House route and art pass. It retains the existing five chapters.

- World work, including the school drainage pump, takes prompt priority over a
  co-located cache. A full cache no longer masks another action; its supplies
  remain available when the pack has room.
- Pause and inventory freeze the clock that drives the searchlight.
- Rehearsal saves and the main Continue state are kept separate. A rehearsal
  cannot manufacture a Continue entry for a nonexistent main journey.
- Save types are validated before applying state. A last-good backup is retained
  and used after primary corruption. Failed writes/renames produce a notice.
- Medicine is mapped to controller LB as well as keyboard H.
- Guarded clipped terrain cut faces, positive turf thickness and fully folded
  limb/cloth outlines avoid invalid polygons encountered during full traversal.

Save isolation: this build uses Godot user:// under WhenTheSkyClears/v041.
Earlier original/v040 saves were preserved. Native disk tests use unique
disposable paths. Copying an earlier valid schema-1 journey into this build is
supported by validation; no existing personal journey was present to migrate.

Scope status: these are initial functional fixes. Pair cover positioning,
individual exposure, painted presentation, authored physical transitions,
The Last House Before the Crossing route, sound and accessibility work remain
in the approved development plan. This checkpoint does not claim that they are
finished, nor that scripted checks establish human controller feel or AAA quality.

Evidence is in evidence/v041. Exact tested binary identity and check counts are
recorded in CHECKPOINT_MANIFEST.json once packaged verification is complete.
