WHEN THE SKY CLEARS / 0.5.3 / GIVING EACH OTHER ROOM

This is a bounded Last House motion, composition and decision-feedback pass.
No chapter, character bitmap, collision size, player movement tuning or save
schema/directory was added or replaced. Existing painted identities are retained.

CHANGED
The companion anticipates a close grounded reversal and gives room using normal
body acceleration. He can briefly travel ahead, check back and then rejoin; there
is no position lock, teleport or force applied to the girl. Yielding is disabled
when waiting, crouching into cover, helping at the support, airborne, blocked by
a gate or unable to find safe ground. The player's initial matched reversal
positions are byte-for-byte identical as serialized by the native baseline/after
fixture. This establishes no added displacement delay in that fixture; it is not
a physical input-latency or controller-feel measurement.

The faster reversals exposed an existing foot-lock defect: a direction change
could declare the lifted swing foot planted in midair. The gathering step now
uses the grounded foot and its remaining travel. Planted soles, actual hip/knee/
ankle chains and hand targets are measured separately from inspected captures.
Crouching sits lower behind cover; stationary children attend to the light.
Relief uses lowered shoulders, softened knees and inward attention after the
shared meal. They remain equal standing height and their colliders are unchanged.

Wet clothing/face treatment is restrained and local. Sparse floorboard contact
seams, actual footfall ripples and wet patches respond to the existing room/floor
layout. Painted shelf rubble/timbers are retained with small joinery accents.
Two generic flat bench props were removed because the painting already contains
chairs. Distant village/goal-house values recede more quietly behind the actors.
These are authored 2D responses, not physical rain/reflections or new art assets.

Upper-shelf versus sheltered-arcade choices state risk/reward at their approach.
The awning describes its one-salvage benefit, and cover readiness duplicates the
individual safety labels. Quiet completion waits for the meal gesture, not an
extra five-second gate. Warmth/drying still recover over time while the room stays
playable. The route-end wetness expectation is <.2 instead of <.1 because the old
check assumed that forced wait; independent optional drying checks are retained.
No timer was lengthened and no human 5-10 minute pacing claim is made.

VERIFICATION / REVIEW
The matched native fixed-input fixture includes quick reversals, doorway travel,
cover stops, reciprocal support, bag/meal/relief and composition views. Starts are
deliberately reset between those cases; it is not a continuous journey. Evidence
records exact source/build hashes, movement/contact probes and actual captures.
Both Last House routes are also captured continuously at30fps, normal simulated
speed, using actual input/interaction logic. Movies are silent verification media;
they do not establish human controller, sound or artistic acceptance. Regression
counts are functional evidence and are not an animation/composition quality score.
A small QA-only audio cleanup interval allows stopped streams to release before
fast headless shutdown; normal sound mix/behavior is retained from v052.

REMAINING QUALITY LIMITS
The articulated painted cutouts still need a dedicated artist/animator's judgment
on profiles, knee/elbow shapes, weight and cloth transitions. Tight blocked/waiting
or airborne crossings can still occlude the children; safe yielding intentionally
does not push someone off a ledge or override authored cooperation/cover. The
repeated house and shelf imagery remains recognizable despite restrained staging
changes. No completed external Godot motion export was available or integrated.
No subjective hearing, physical controller, Mac runtime or human pacing study was
performed. v052 sound/input/accessibility limitations remain. The Library upload
connection remains previously denied; no retry/bypass or Library ID is asserted.

prior builds, saves and Unreal/archive paths are preserved. The existing schema1
