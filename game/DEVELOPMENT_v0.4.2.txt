TWO-CHILD WOODS PUZZLE
The existing woods route now has an optional counterweighted footbridge before
the mill. Bring the boy onto the near pressure cradle and ask him to wait [Q].
His physical weight lowers the hinged span. Wait for it to settle, cross, and
step onto the far iron tooth. A different child must still hold the cradle for
the ratchet to latch. Then call him across [Q] and search the keeper's medicine.
Calling too early releases the cradle; the span returns above the safe lower
road. Real bridge collision and child-floor contacts drive the sequence.
This version keeps the existing five chapters and 79,200 world-unit route.

CHARACTERS AND WORLD
The girl has a dedicated painted coat/sleeve/boot atlas mapped to her rig,
preserving her established head, hair and blue ribbon. Pumping, bracing,
clearing, searching, warming and balancing use distinct work poses. Both
children's hands follow the moving pump grips. Physical cradle contact starts
a balance pose; walking off releases it without action locking. Turn follow-
through, landing anticipation, cold hands and quiet care develop their acting.
The native pump fixture measured maximum grip errors of 1.091 world pixels
for the girl and 1.045 for the boy, with zero stationary body drift during that
fixture. The labelled normal-speed review is available; artistic acceptance
remains separate.

above a child beneath it; a far latch preserves the open state. Painted
cradles, pulleys, ropes and opposed weights follow the physical displacement.
Generic barriers use timber slats, iron straps and support guides. Nearby
grass responds to physical contact. The painted tent remains the material
clarity benchmark for the new clothing and mechanism parts.

Finite food/salvage/medicine, companion protection, cover, listening lanes,

BUILD AND VERIFY
Open project.godot in Godot 4.6 Standard and use F5. Editor play needs no
templates or Python. From the repository root, after source is frozen:
  python game/tools/setup_godot.py
  python game/tools/build_v042.py export
  python game/tools/build_v042.py test all
  python game/tools/build_v042.py test capture
  python game/tools/build_v042.py verify

Outputs stay under build/v0.4.2 and evidence/v042. Export temporarily selects
portable Windows templates/product metadata, restoring the preset byte for
byte. Identity hashes runtime source, assets and UID sidecars. Native checks
must match the exact executable. Source animation is a separate fixture with
overlapping baseline checks, excluded from the native functional total.

New suites are --sluice-checks, --craft-checks and --world-craft-checks, alongside
retained suites and continuous traversal. They cover bridge cooperation/reset,
rendered work contacts, pause/release, gate collision/safety and persistence.
Targeted initial placements are separate from campaign navigation.

Capture uses --capture-v042 for 16 required native 4K PNGs. New views are
13_counterweight_wait.png, 14_counterweight_crossing.png,
15_counterweight_reunion.png and 16_logging_counterweight.png. The last view
stages the moving logging gate at partial height with the boy on its cradle.
Fresh sluice_capture.json identifies only views 13-15. Capture fails immediately
if the wait, crossing and reunion sequence states were not reached.
