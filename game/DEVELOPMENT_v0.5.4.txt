WHEN THE SKY CLEARS / 0.5.4 / PAINTED SILHOUETTES AND OCCUPIED ROOMS

A visual milestone in Last House, based on fd7520d. The original painted atlas
already contains coats, sleeves, trousers, hands and boots. Continuous UV strips
now bend that retained paint with the established rig, replacing flat tubes,
boots and seam lines in this slice. The girl's long coat/skirt and blue ribbon,
the boy's short coat/olive trousers, original profiles and equal body heights
remain. Original bitmap/audio bytes are unchanged. No new art rights claim.

Near shoulders follow the painted armhole. Authored wrist contacts remain on
the support, partner and carried bag. A small passing lean and depth ordering
make crossings easier to read. A grounded following boy can yield during a safe
air crossing; wait commands and unsafe-ground refusal remain in force.

The first house has a cold partial interior and an abandoned handcart; the damaged
passage uses the retained collapsed frontage, so it no longer repeats the final
kitchen. Sparse stocked shelf details, the painted photograph and the intact chairs
and table distinguish abandonment, risk and relief. No arbitrary waits,
new chapter, shelter-management system or input-response delay was added.

VERIFICATION

LIMITS
This is a retained-paint deformation/composition pass, not newly hand-painted
frame animation. Profile flips, some extreme joint folds and exact co-location
while one child waits can still read as cutout animation or overlap. No external
motion export is integrated in this milestone. Wider campaign art is unchanged.
No human 5-10 minute pacing, physical-controller feel, subjective listening or
Mac runtime validation is claimed. Godot4.6 Standard source remains portable.
