A CONNECTED MILL ENCOUNTER
The woods now end with a connected bridge, fence, wheel, tether and gate.
The bridge deck spans a real collision gap. The fence and boards provide
physical steps onto the large wheel; the construction is part of the route.
Standing on its crown loads the frame for 0.4 seconds, then begins a 1.2-second
warning before the wheel breaks free. Its RigidBody2D body rolls to the right.
The visible tether and gate respond to its actual travel. After 240 pixels
of travel the gate can rise clear. Holding E does not bypass the mechanism.

Both children must retreat from the rolling wheel. A child left in front can
be hurt, so sending the girl ahead while forgetting him has consequences.
The approach and retreat ground provide room to plan the movement. This is
an authored physical sequence; other painted objects are not automatically
interactive merely because they appear in the environment.

ROUTES WITH DIFFERENT COSTS
The five chapters span 79,200 authored world units, up from 56,000. This is
measured route data, not a human playtime estimate. New mill gardens, canal,
signal yard, shattered arcade and living terraces extend the existing story.
Read JOURNEY_v0.4.0.txt for chapter staging and route details.

The woods canal and school arcade each offer real lower and upper routes.
The low route is continuous ground under cold water and a listening lane.
A slow crouched crossing can avoid detection while saving salvage. Faster
wading splashes; running carries farther. A shared pump costs one salvage
and drains the low route. Both children must help with that work.

The high route uses stepped, one-way platforms above the lower hazard.
Both children can climb. One salvage secures the upper crossbar; dry food
and medicine lie beyond it. The approach salvage can pay for the pump OR
the upper brace. Inventory carried from earlier choices can change the plan.
The routes meet again on safe ground. A child left below stays exposed.

There are 18 timed hazard lanes, including two sound-sensitive listeners,
and 27 held relay, clearing and bracing actions across the authored levels.
Moving light, falling masonry, wind, shelter repairs and finite searches
remain part of the journey. Quiet intervals and permanent repairs offer
different ways through selected hazards.

SURVIVAL AND THE PACK
The pack holds four food portions, three salvage pieces and two medicines.
Across the complete world, caches contain 26 food portions, 18 salvage and
13 medicines. Those are finite authored totals, not starting inventory or
guaranteed rewards on the main route. Full packs leave supplies in place.
Optional elevated searches often reward medicine instead of another meal.
Warmth, wetness, hunger and shared strength still affect the journey.

CHARACTERS AND FEEL
The drawn children retain their established profiles and her blue ribbon.
Crouching now blends into the pose instead of snapping immediately. Standing
checks overhead clearance so a low ceiling keeps the child down. Turn braking,
support-foot locks, quiet settling and sloped boot placement refine movement.
The existing articulated walk, run, landing, mantle, shared work, fatigue and
companion gestures remain editable in the native controller.

LOOK AND SOUND
The painted tent's readable material is the benchmark for this art pass.
Faces and clothing keep visible painted detail, with clearer skin, hair and
garment values. Material-aware tints and softened contact shadows improve
consistency across children, terrain, cover and props. Environmental darkness
is restrained so it supports the staging without hiding the character work.
This is a visual revision to review, not an artistic approval or AAA claim.

Collision-matched terrain, painted banks, roots and foreground cover continue
the layered world. The new canal layer is assets/environments/Canal_v040.png;
its recorded prompt and provenance are beside it. Mill_Background_v040.png
removes the moving parts from the architectural painting. Mill_Parts_v040.png
keeps the wheel, gate and railing in separate atlas cells. The runtime uses
those parts with their corresponding collision bodies and mechanism state.
Each painting has its built-in ImageGen prompt and provenance beside it;
the generated source pixels and earlier paintings are preserved.
Vulkan Forward Mobile uses 4x 2D MSAA and mipmapped paintings. Material-specific
Kenney CC0 foot contacts remain; movement noise now also affects listeners.

CONTROLS

Hold E: search, work or rest | Hold E+A/D against crate: push
Q: ask him to wait low / call him | H: medicine | Tab: pack/objective
R: checkpoint recovery | Esc: pause | F11: fullscreen | M: mute
Controller: left stick/D-pad move, A jump, B crouch, X held work,
Y wait/call, RB run, Back pack, Start pause.
