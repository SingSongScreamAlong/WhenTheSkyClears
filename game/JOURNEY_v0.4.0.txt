WHEN THE SKY CLEARS — JOURNEY DESIGN 0.4.0

The five chapters now span 79,200 authored world units, up from 56,000.
Length alone is not the goal. The new sectors ask how the two children will
get through, what they will spend, and which risks they will carry together.

TWO PHYSICAL FORKS
The woods canal and shattered school arcade each contain two playable levels
of terrain. The lower way is continuous ground, but it holds cold water and
passes a listening post. A slow crouched crossing can remain quiet without
spending salvage. Walking through water or rushing risks detection during
the listening interval. A one-salvage pump drains the lower way; both children
must help with the work.

The upper way is a real stepped boardwalk/gallery above the water and the
listening lane. Jumping onto its boards is a choice: one-way platform tops
allow the children to stay on the ground underneath. Both children can climb.
A crossbar blocks only the upper passage and takes one salvage to secure.
Beyond it, dry food and medicine reward the climb. A salvage piece in the
approach can drain the low way OR open the high one. It cannot pay both costs.
Existing inventory from earlier decisions may change what is possible.

These are physical route alternatives, not a menu or a cosmetic raised strip.
The upper route's foot height is outside the lower hazard's vertical bounds.
Water also checks actual feet, so the high route remains dry. The routes
rejoin on safe ground. No compulsory death gap forces a route selection.

NEW SITUATIONS BY CHAPTER
VILLAGE: terraced mill gardens, an exposed floodbank, a coat worth searching
for medicine, a beam needing four hands, and a mill roof that can consume
salvage or be crossed between falls. The roots at the mill exit take time.

WOODS: the drainage mouth opens into the first route choice. Below are water,
cold and the listening post. Above are the towing-path boards and a dry
medicine case. The signal hut offers warmth after either crossing; sleepers
block the road beyond it. Keeping the medicine dry is an environmental beat.
Beyond the hut is a physical mill encounter. Its bridge deck spans an actual
gap; the fence rails, wheel and gate belong to the same staged construction.
Two board steps lead onto the wheel. A child's weight strains the frame,
then starts a warning before the wheel breaks free and rolls to the right.
The moving wheel pulls the visible tether, and the gate rises only after
240 pixels of real wheel travel. There is no held switch that opens it.
Both children must retreat out of the wheel's path. The ground beneath the
wheel remains continuous, with clear room behind it and beyond the gate.

CAMP: a drainage way leads below the freight road, then into the signal yard.
The light can be crossed in its quiet interval. Alternatively, the children
can stop together at the signal crank and permanently turn it away. A supply
box is exposed before that decision. The broken station arcade leads onward.

SCHOOL: the kitchen approach offers another scarce salvage piece. The broken
arcade repeats the canal decision in a longer, different space: a flooded
ground gallery under a listening window, or a dry nurse's satchel in the
upper gallery. A damaged greenhouse and living soil follow the reunion point.

ASH: new living terraces rise and fall after the previous final approach.
Wind catches the high ground. A fallen garden trellis takes both children,
and another greenhouse beam can be made safe before the last doorway. The
final interaction remains taking his hand; it now stands at x=13,900.

SUPPLIES AND CONSEQUENCES
Across the complete authored journey, caches contain 26 food portions,
18 salvage pieces and 13 medicines. These are finite world totals, not
starting inventory or guaranteed main-route rewards. Food is concentrated
at selected refuges; many tool searches supply only salvage, and the six
earlier raised spurs now usually reward medicine. The first legacy caches
and the camp's three accessible pre-door salvage pieces are preserved.

New approach caches and sheltered fires support the added distance, while
packs remain finite. The salvage pack holds three pieces. Medicine and two
food portions on each high route compete with the effort,
time and salvage needed to get there. Cold water can turn a saved salvage
piece into a later need for food or medicine. Leaving the boy below while
the girl searches above leaves him exposed; his route must be planned too.

BUILD AND REVIEW BOUNDARY
The deterministic authoring script reads previous/v0.3.0/levels and preserves
earlier encounter positions. It validates terrain slopes below 18 degrees,
route metadata, finite cache IDs, interaction ordering and timing windows.
These are data checks. Native physics, both branch traversals, survival and
listening consequences need their separate runtime evidence. Route length is
measured data, not a claim of human playtime, balance or AAA completion.
