PLAY

THIS PASS
The children now use a larger, authored side-view rig. Stride phase comes from
actual travel. Feet lock to the world during stance; knees solve to the feet.
Stops, breathing, crouch, running, landing compression, garment movement and
ribbon overlap are authored separately. Work gestures distinguish a wheel/pump,
overhead roof repair and resting at a stove. The prior runtime used generic
projected motion over pasted body parts; that controller is preserved separately.
Imported animation/model sources remain available as references for later craft.

Walking has acceleration and a faster stop; running spends stamina. Buffered
jumps and coyote time remain. A ledge pull-up catches reachable edges through
native collision movement. The follower can use the same traversal mechanism.

Painted foreground artwork replaces many plain blocks: rubble, wall cover,
logs, supplies, carts, stoves, winches, tents and doorway forms. Ground has
painted soil and roots. Near cover occludes the children during stealth.
Atmosphere uses gradual haze; water animates down after pumping. Character and
paint textures use mipmaps and 2D multisample antialiasing for cleaner edges.

Kenney's CC0 Impact Sounds now provides five variants each for grass, concrete
and wood footsteps. Actual foot contacts select the surface and trigger foley.
The publisher archive and license are retained locally; the used clips and
license are in assets/audio/foley. Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

REVISED ENCOUNTERS
Village: a movable crate beside the old masonry. Hold E and push with A/D.
Forest: a washed-out stream too wide to jump; lower the bridge at the winch.
The logging gate can be held by the waiting boy or by a physically pushed crate.
Camp: find salvage, clear/brace the shelter entrance, then choose whether to
spend another piece patching the leaking roof. An open shelter can still be wet.
School: the flooded yard slows movement and soaks clothes. Work the drainage
pump to open the route; hold both children in cover when warning cues arrive.
Ash: accumulated survival state, a last rest and the reunion at the doorway.

SEARCHING AND WORK NOW TAKE TIME
Hold E; the ring above the girl shows progress. Letting go cancels the action.
The girl stops moving while working, and damage interrupts it. This makes the
timing and position of a search, wheel, repair, pump or rest matter.
Food, warmth, wet clothing, finite pack capacity, medicine and exhaustion remain.
Sheltering only one child is insufficient. Low cover requires crouching.

EVIDENCE
evidence/v020/packaged_*/ contains results from the Windows executable.
The continuous route uses actual held interactions, native movement, finite
supplies, gates and recovery. It records encounter completions and any failure.
The new encounter checks exercise native crate pushing, plate weight, interrupted
winch work, bridge collision, flooded movement, pump operation and roof repair.

WTSC_Movement_Study_v020.mp4 is a silent 12-second, 30 fps development motion
study on an explicitly isolated flat route, using the actual controller/renderer.
It demonstrates walk, stop, crouch, run, jump/land and low cover pose. It is not
a campaign playthrough. All 360 frames decode. The sampled stance feet have
zero world-X drift in that study; this does not establish final animation quality.

The generated foreground kit, unchanged source pixels and exact ImageGen prompt
are retained in assets/props/PROVENANCE_v020.txt. The actual output is 1254 square.
The original environment paintings and all trailer production files remain intact.

QUALITY BOUNDARY
This is a substantial development iteration, not a final quality claim. It still
needs human playtesting and more distinctive child acting, tighter costume and
hand shapes, richer transitions, authored terrain/collision matching, deeper
encounter variation, and a longer campaign. Grounded foot contact and technical
checks do not by themselves make the animation expressive or the game AAA.
