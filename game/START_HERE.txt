WHEN THE SKY CLEARS - DEVELOPMENT 0.3.0
19 October 2026

PLAY
PLAY.cmd opens build/v0.3.0/When the Sky Clears.exe.
Extract the Windows ZIP before running its executable. No engine installation
is needed to play. The editable source is the Godot project in this folder.
Existing v0.1.0 and v0.2.0 builds and full snapshots remain in the original
workspace. The editable ZIP contains the current project and prior level data.
This version keeps its own journey_v3.json in the existing Godot user folder.
Previous journey files are preserved. Continue restores actual saved survival
resources; checkpoint recovery is a separate action.

CHARACTERS AND FEEL
The two drawn children retain their established profiles and the blue ribbon.
Companion glances, warning gestures, cold and fatigue posture, pushing effort,
distinct rising/falling/mantling poses, cloth and braid follow-through, boot
and hand shapes add character to the distance-driven planted-foot controller.
Both actors stop for shared work. Hands meet at a reachable common height;
they release while airborne or working. A waiting companion stays low.

Ground profiles drive native collision polygons and the painted surface.
Planted feet sample that same surface, so soles stay on a hill instead of
floating above a decorative slope. Recovery clears old jump and climb state.
Wading applies only when feet actually reach the water.

A LONGER AUTHORED JOURNEY
The five chapters now span 56,000 world units, up from 27,600. This is authored
route length, not a human playtime estimate. Orchard ridges, a rail path and
goods station, the freight yard, school courtyards and the ash garden give the
journey new situations. Six elevated supply spurs reward exploration.
Ten new timed lanes and sixteen held work actions extend the earlier encounters.
Read JOURNEY_v0.3.0.txt for the chapter design.

Both children must be together to lift a jammed barrier. Clearing debris costs
time and effort. A roof brace costs one real salvage and permanently stops its
falling-masonry lane; waiting for the quiet interval offers another way through.
Moving light can detect either child, including one left behind. Falling dust
and roof creaks warn of debris. Crosswinds reduce exposed movement and spend
stamina; crouching steadies both children. Safe approaches fit both children.
Finite supply caches and sheltered fires support the longer journey.

LOOK AND SOUND
The existing layered paintings are preserved. A new transparent painted railway
station layer, grounded dressing, soil strata, roots, broken banks, varied zone
details and foreground cover extend the visual world. The new painting is in
assets/environments/Rail_Yard_v030.png; its exact built-in ImageGen prompt and
provenance are in assets/environments/Rail_Yard_PROVENANCE_v030.txt.
Vulkan Forward Mobile renders with 4x 2D MSAA and mipmapped paintings.
Kenney CC0 footsteps from v0.2.0 remain, now with quieter companion contacts.

CONTROLS
A/D or arrows: move | Space/W/Up: jump | Shift: run | S/Down: crouch
Hold E: search, work or rest | Hold E+A/D against crate: push
Q: ask him to wait low / call him | H: medicine | Tab: pack/objective
R: checkpoint recovery | Esc: pause | F11: fullscreen | M: mute
Controller: left stick/D-pad move, A jump, B crouch, X held work,
Y wait/call, RB run, Back pack, Start pause.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
Evidence is retained in evidence/v030. Continuous journey navigation uses real
Input and native physics, held interactions, finite supplies and actual recovery.
Targeted tests deliberately arrange isolated scenarios to measure slopes,
sole contact, separate-child exposure, wind, work costs and save persistence.
Optional spur checks are separate from the continuous main-route traversal.
Character close views and movement studies are labeled as studies; 4K gameplay
captures use the actual renderer. Technical tests do not establish artistic or
human playtest acceptance.

This is an expanded development build, with a compact campaign still requiring
more bespoke acting, animation transitions, terrain/prop craft, encounter depth,
sound design and human balancing to reach the requested AAA production target.
It is not presented as a finished commercial game.
