WHEN THE SKY CLEARS — JOURNEY DESIGN 0.3.0

The five story chapters now span 56,000 world units, compared with 27,600 in
the previous build. Existing encounters retain their coordinates. The extra
route continues each chapter's environment rather than repeating it as a new
chapter. The ending has moved from 5,400 to 10,500 in the final chapter.

VILLAGE: the first road now reaches an orchard ridge, a crooked bar that needs
both children, an exposed wind lane, a raised supply terrace, a fallen bough,
and a shed whose roof can be secured with a scarce piece of salvage.

WOODS: a high ridge and optional supply shelf lead to an abandoned rail path.
The hanging loading roof can be braced or crossed between falls. A shared
rail fence lift leads into the wind gully and an obstructed culvert.

CAMP: after the original shelter, sacks block the freight entrance. The
waiting-room beam threatens the route. A carriage door needs four hands;
the first-aid case above its luggage rack rewards looking up. A sweeping
light guards the final freight yard before the rubble-filled station exit.

SCHOOL: the original flood still matters. Beyond it are a fallen classroom
shelf, a sports-cupboard supply spur, a damaged library awning, a hollow in
the courtyard, desks that must be shifted, and a watched road out. Small
environmental memories carry the school story without conversations.

ASH: the previous last doorway becomes the start of another stretch. A
garden gate is tangled in wire. The children wait out the ash wind, search
an optional ledge, lift a fallen branch, and find a little green beneath the
ash before reaching the final hand-taking interaction.

The new route includes 10 timed hazard lanes and 16 held physical actions.
Each lane has space for both children in cover at its entry and exit. Quiet
windows are longer than a walking crossing with the follower. Roof braces
are optional and consume one salvage; waiting for a safe window remains a
route through. Relays require both children, while clearing consumes time.
Finite food, useful salvage and sheltered fires precede the longer chains.
There are six optional elevated supply spurs, reached by two manageable
steps; these are rewards rather than mandatory jumps.

Terrain profiles add low ridges and shallow gullies with slopes below 18
degrees. New mandatory terrain is continuous: no unseen compulsory death
gap has been added. Checkpoints are on the calm approaches and safe exits.

The length figure is measured route data, not a playtime claim. Automated
completion verifies reachability; feel, encounter staging and visual craft
still require further play and revision toward the requested quality.
