Development 0.4.4 adds three physical puzzle routes and improves the painted children's animation.

- Orchard: push a heavy crate onto an iron chock to raise a hinged ramp, then bring both children to the supplies above.
- Freight lift: ask the boy to wait on the cargo deck, work the winch, climb the loading steps and reunite. Lowering stops if either child is underneath.
- Garden: board both children, crouch beneath the ferry's canvas and crank across the canal. Releasing the reel brakes the real deck; the cold-water bypass remains available.
- New painted head angles, anticipatory turns, moving-platform foot contact and more grounded pump, rail and ferry work. Original painted profiles remain preserved.
- 557 native functional checks passed across 19 suites, plus a separate 27-check source animation fixture. Continuous automated traversal completed all five chapters with one checkpoint recovery and no blocking failure.
- 27 native 4K captures and three fully decoded, labelled native motion studies accompany the evidence. These are automated checks and bounded reviews; human balancing, artistic acceptance and AAA finish remain active development work.

The Windows ZIP includes ORCHARD_PLAYTEST.cmd, FREIGHT_LIFT_PLAYTEST.cmd and GARDEN_FERRY_PLAYTEST.cmd for isolated, unsaved rehearsals. Run When the Sky Clears.exe for the campaign. A/D move, Space jump, S crouch, hold E work, E plus movement push, Q wait/call, Esc pause.

Editable source: open project.godot in Godot 4.6 Standard. Godot and Python are not needed for the Windows build. Prior releases remain available.
